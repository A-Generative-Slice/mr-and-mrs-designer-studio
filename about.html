Our Story & Philosophy

The Mr & Mrs Modern Atelier

Founded on the conviction that Indian haute couture should never suffer from ill-fitting measurements, fragile threads, or painful 45-minute saree draping marathons.

Our Guiding Principle

"Slow Fashion Precision, Radical Body Geometry."

Most commercial studios force your body into generic template blocks. At Mr & Mrs Modern (operated legally under Mr & Mrs Modern), every garment begins with anatomical posture drafting.

We calibrate embroidery stitch vectors directly to your armhole circumference and bust apex so no motif is ever sliced off during assembly. We treat needlework as both fine art and structural engineering.

Milestones

Atelier Heritage & Achievements

A progression from traditional handloom embellishment to a state-of-the-art hybrid production atelier.

Foundational Phase

Master Hand Embroidery

Began as specialized bridal artisans mastering ancient zardozi, kasab, and French bullion needlecraft on Kanchipuram and Banarasi silks.

Engineering Innovation

The 90-Second Saree Patent

Engineered our calibrated waist-anchor system that eliminated safety pins, allowing brides to step into heavy silk sarees in under 90 seconds without sagging.

Industrial Expansion

In-House DTF & Sublimation

Installed industrial multi-head computerized embroidery machines and high-density 1200 DPI Direct-to-Film transfer units for commercial bulk manufacturing.

Legal Compliance

GST Corporate Integration

Fully registered entity (Mr & Mrs Modern) providing corporate clients, hotels, and designers with full Input Tax Credit (ITC) compliant commercial invoicing.

Production Standards

The Atelier Machinery & Tooling

We combine old-world hand tools with computerized German and Japanese precision equipment.

Wilcom Digitization Lab

Every sketch is converted into a vector coordinate map calibrating stitch angle, underlay density, and pull compensation to prevent silk puckering.

Multi-Needle Computerized Array

High-speed multi-head automated embroidery executing metallic zari bullion stitches at 1,000 stitches per minute with laser thread breakage sensors.

Ultrasonic Steam Pleating Tables

Deep vacuum steam presses calibrate pleat depth to the exact millimeter, locking knife-edges in place while keeping delicate silk threads unharmed.

Hydraulic Heat-Transfer Fusion

Pneumatic uniform-pressure heat press arrays that fuse polyurethane DTF adhesives into garment cotton fibers for 60+ wash commercial durability.
