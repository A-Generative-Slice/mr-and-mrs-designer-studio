Client Privacy Policy

How Mr & Mrs Designer Studio protects your anatomical fitting measurements, bespoke bridal designs, and personal information with utmost discretion.

1. Confidentiality of Anatomical Fit Profiles

When you visit our Perungudi atelier in Chennai for private body measurements or submit measurement profiles online, this data is strictly restricted to master pattern makers and senior tailors. We never sell, share, or monetize client anatomical data under any circumstances.

2. Wedding Trousseau Design Secrecy

We understand the sacred nature of wedding ensembles. Bespoke bridal embroidery artwork, blouse motifs, and lehenga designs are kept strictly confidential until your celebration date. Photos of your custom ensembles are only showcased on our portfolio with explicit client written permission.

3. Secure Cloud Records & Supabase Storage

Client appointment records, invoice receipts, and fitting histories are stored in enterprise-grade encrypted databases. All financial transactions are processed through certified Indian banking gateways adhering to RBI compliance and 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. Doorstep Valet Courier Sharing

For pre-pleated saree boxes and dispatched trousseau orders, your name, delivery address, and phone number are shared solely with our verified insured logistics partners (BlueDart / DTDC) for the singular purpose of doorstep transit.

5. Atelier Grievance Officer & Discretion Desk

For any inquiries regarding your stored fit records, data removal requests, or confidentiality queries, please write directly to our atelier concierge at atelier@mrandmrsmodern.com or visit our Perungudi studio in Chennai.
