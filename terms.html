Terms of Service & Studio SLA

Bespoke craftsmanship guidelines, fitting trial protocols, bridal trousseau timelines, and commercial standards governing all commissions at Mr & Mrs Designer Studio.

1. Commission Agreement & Design Freeze

Every bespoke couture ensemble, bridal embroidery commission, or custom tailoring order begins with a private measurement consultation. Once fabric selection, artwork placement, and thread specifications are signed off, the design is frozen. Any subsequent silhouette modifications requested after fabric cutting has begun will incur material recalibration charges.

2. Advance Deposits & Milestones

A minimum 50% commitment advance is required upon commission initiation to secure raw silk yardage, genuine zari metal threads, and artisan frame allocation. The remaining balance is payable upon successful fitting trial approval prior to studio dispatch or in-person collection.

3. Saree Pre-Pleating Zero-Damage Guarantee

Our proprietary pre-pleating valet uses non-invasive steam calibration and cotton-padded soft micro-grippers. We guarantee zero thread rupture and zero needle tear to heirloom Kanchipuram, Banarasi, and Organza silks. Every pre-pleated drape is dispatched in our signature crush-proof magnetic box.

4. Fitting Trials & Alteration Policy

Standard bespoke garments include one complimentary basting trial and one final adjustment trial within 14 days of delivery. All garments are constructed with a generous 2.5-inch inner seam allowance to accommodate anatomical changes over time.

5. Turnaround & Express Atelier Craft

Standard production timelines range from 3 to 7 business days depending on handcraft density. Express turnaround (24 to 48 hours) is available subject to dedicated artisan frame availability and an express calibration premium.

6. Commercial Billing & GST Compliance

All commercial transactions are invoiced with valid Indian GST documentation under GSTIN: 29AAMFM8921C1Z4. Corporate and bulk uniform clients are issued GST B2B tax invoices with eligible input tax credit (ITC).
